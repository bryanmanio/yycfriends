YYCfriends privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

YYCfriends has no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your location never leaves your phone. The app does send anonymous usage counts to a server run by its developer, to see which parts of it people use.

Your location stays on your phone

Location is used on the device only: to sort businesses by how close they are and to show where you are on the map. It is never transmitted anywhere, and it is never stored. The app asks for it only when you tap the map's locate button or the button in Settings, and only while you are using the app.

Usage analytics

The app sends a short message to an analytics server (Umami, run by the developer at analytics.bryanmanio.com) when you do certain things in it:

  • open the app, along with the app's version and the version of the business list;
  • open a business's card, and which business it was;
  • turn on a category, area or "New" filter;
  • search the list for something that matches nothing, including the words you searched;
  • hide a business, or open one in Maps;
  • log a saving, including the amount and the business;
  • see the welcome screen, tap its buttons, or tap "Join as a business";
  • answer the location permission prompt (yes or no, never the location itself).

Each message carries a random identifier made by the app the first time it runs. It is not your Apple ID, your advertising identifier or anything else that names you or your phone, and deleting the app discards it. It lets the developer count people rather than app launches.

Like any server, the analytics server sees the internet address your request comes from. It uses it to estimate your country and city and does not store the address itself. It also records your iOS version and language.

This information is used only to understand how the app is used and to improve it. It is not linked to your identity, not used for advertising, not combined with data from anywhere else, and not sold or shared with anyone.

The list of businesses

Twice a day at most, the app checks a public GitHub repository for a newer list of Friends Card businesses and downloads it if there is one. The request carries nothing about you. GitHub sees an internet address, as any website would, and its own privacy policy covers what it does with that.

Your savings log

Savings you log are stored on your phone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account, so they follow you to your other devices. The developer cannot read your iCloud data. The amount and business of each new saving are also sent to the analytics server as described above.

What is stored on your phone

The businesses you have hidden, your savings log, your sort, filter and appearance choices, the analytics identifier, and a copy of the business list. Deleting the app deletes them, apart from the savings log's copy in your iCloud.

Open in Maps

"Open in Maps" hands a business's location to Apple Maps, which is a separate app covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app has no content directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.
