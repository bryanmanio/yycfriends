YYCfriends privacy policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

YYCfriends collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server that belongs to this app.

Your location stays on your phone

Location is used on the device only: to sort businesses by how close they are, to draw the map, and to let iOS update your Apple Wallet card when you arrive somewhere that takes the Friends Card. It is never transmitted anywhere, and it is never stored.

Opening hours and ratings

When you open a business, the app asks Google Places for that one business's opening hours and rating, identifying it by a Google place identifier. The request carries nothing about you and nothing about where you are, and nothing it returns is saved on your device. Google's own privacy policy covers what Google does with that request.

What is stored on your phone

The businesses you have hidden, and your sort and filter choices. They live on your device and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes them.

The Wallet card

The Wallet passes ship inside the app, already signed. Adding one, and swapping it as you move around the city, happens entirely on your device through Apple Wallet.

Reporting an error

The "Report an error" link at the bottom of a business opens an email addressed to yycfriends@bryanmanio.com, which you write and send yourself. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Children

The app has no content directed at children, and it collects nothing from anyone of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.
