YYCfriends

Every business that takes the CJSW Friends Card, on one map, with an Apple Wallet card that names the discount before you get to the till.

Get help

Email yycfriends@bryanmanio.com. If the problem is one business, the "Report an error" link at the bottom of that business's page in the app fills in its name for you, which makes it much faster to fix.

A listing is wrong, or the shop has closed

The list comes from CJSW's own published list of Friends Card businesses, which turns over once a year with the funding drive, so a business can move or close between updates. Report it and it gets corrected in the next build.

The map does not know where I am

The app needs location permission to sort businesses by distance. Open the iOS Settings app, find YYCfriends, then Location, and choose "While Using the App" at least. Without it the list falls back to alphabetical order and still works.

The Wallet card is not changing as I move

Keeping the card current in the background needs "Always" location, which iOS will only offer after you have granted "While Using the App". The app asks for it from Settings, then Location, inside the app. Choosing "While Using the App" instead is fine: everything else works, and the card simply stays on whatever it last showed.

Where are the hours and the rating?

They are fetched live from Google when you open a business, so they need a connection, and a business Google does not have a listing for shows none.
