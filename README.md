# YYCfriends

Support and privacy pages for **YYCfriends**, an iOS app for actually using a CJSW Friends
Card: every participating business on one map, sorted by distance, with an Apple Wallet card
that names the discount before you reach the till.

- **Privacy policy:** [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md), or [privacy.html](privacy.html) once Pages
  is on
- **Support:** [index.html](index.html), or email yycfriends@bryanmanio.com

The app's source is not in this repository. This one exists so the App Store listing has a
public support URL and a public privacy policy URL to point at.

To serve these as a site: Settings, then Pages, then Deploy from a branch, `main`, `/ (root)`.
They then appear at `https://bryanmanio.github.io/yycfriends/` and
`https://bryanmanio.github.io/yycfriends/privacy.html`.

YYCfriends is not affiliated with CJSW 90.9 FM. The Friends Card, and the discounts it
carries, belong to the station. Buy the card from
[the funding drive](https://fundingdrive.ca/products/35-donation).
